# Pink Panic — asset licences

Every delivered asset, its source and its licence. **Nothing here carries a
non-commercial or share-alike condition, and nothing requires an attribution
string.** The game can ship monetised as-is.

## Sourced assets

| Covers | Source | Licence | Attribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 37 weapons + 10 attachments | **Quaternius — Ultimate Guns Pack**<br>https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimategun.html<br>mirror: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Guns-Pack-cpgUfI4t2F | **CC0 1.0 / Public Domain** | **None required** |

Quaternius releases his packs as CC0 on both his own site and the poly.pizza
mirror. CC0 is a public-domain dedication: commercial use, modification and
redistribution are all permitted with no conditions and no credit line. One
licence row therefore covers every asset drawn from that pack — the individual
source model for each weapon is named in `MANIFEST.md`.

Attachments sourced from the same pack's accessories folder: `Suppressor`,
`BarrelExtender`, `Choke`, `Scope2x`, `Scope4x`, `SniperScope`, `VerticalGrip`,
`Bipod`, `HeavyStock`, `TacticalLaser`. Plus `Melee_Knife`, from the pack's
bayonet.

## Authored assets

| Covers | Origin | Licence | Attribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 33 attachments + `Melee_Bat` | Authored for this project — no third-party geometry | **Original work, unencumbered** (treat as CC0) | None |

Magazines, rear grips, stocks, most muzzle devices, barrels, lasers and the
iron sights are simple primitive forms. At their triangle budgets and their
on-screen size, authoring them took less time than finding versions with a
licence that survives contact with a monetised game — and it removes the
attribution bookkeeping entirely.

They are deliberately plain. If any of them wants more character — a fatter drum
mag, a stippled texture on the grips, a chunkier suppressor — say which and I
will rework it; they are quick to change.

## What I checked

- Every source model came from a pack whose licence text I read, not from a
  "free download" button.
- No CC-BY, CC-BY-NC, or "free for personal use" asset is present in this
  delivery, so there is nothing to credit and nothing that blocks monetisation.
- No asset is a redistribution of a paid pack.
