## Notes

**Quaternius / Ultimate Guns Pack.** Explicitly released as CC0 / public domain
by the author on both his own site and the poly.pizza mirror. No attribution
string is required, and there are no non-commercial or share-alike conditions,
so it is safe for a monetised game. The pack is 40 weapons, so the same licence
line covers every asset drawn from it.

**RedDot.** Every optic in the CC0 packs I have is a *magnified* rifle scope —
there is no compact red dot in any of them. Rather than pass off a 4x scope as a
red dot, I authored this one: a tube body, hood and eyepiece rings, two turrets,
a rail foot, and a separate `Lens` for the reticle. That also gives it the
cleanest possible licence position — no third party involved at all.

If you would rather every attachment be sourced instead of authored, say so —
but for small parts like this, authoring is usually faster than finding one with
a licence you can actually use, and it removes the attribution bookkeeping.
